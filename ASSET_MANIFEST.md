## 1. Visual Art Direction & Style Guide

### A. Photographic & Illustrative Tone
* **Aesthetic:** Hyper-grounded suburban commercial realism tinged with mild satirical absurdity.
* **Lighting Signature:** Harsh, unyielding 4,000K overhead industrial fluorescent lighting casting faint double-shadows onto high-gloss bleached linoleum.
* **Color Temperature:** Cool commercial whites, sterile beige cinderblock, warehouse corrugated galvanized steel, punctuated by the vibrant corporate blue (`#0055A5`) and safety yellow (`#FFC220`) of MegaMart brand apparel.
* **Aspect Ratios & Resolutions:**
  - **Character Dialogue Portraits:** `512 x 768` (2:3 aspect ratio, bust/waist-up framing with transparent or neutral studio backdrops).
  - **Environment Backgrounds:** `1920 x 1080` (16:9 widescreen landscape, wide-angle lens with authentic depth of field).
  - **POS Battle Avatars & Mini-Game Sprites:** `256 x 256` (1:1 square, sharp pixel/vector line art with high-contrast outlines).
